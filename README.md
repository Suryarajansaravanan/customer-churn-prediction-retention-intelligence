# customer-churn-prediction-retention-intelligence
End-to-end Customer Churn Prediction and Retention Intelligence Analytics project using Python, Machine Learning and Power BI.




## Executive Summary

Customer churn is one of the most significant challenges faced by subscription-based businesses. Acquiring a new customer is often considerably more expensive than retaining an existing one. Therefore, identifying customers at risk of leaving and implementing proactive retention strategies is essential for sustainable business growth.

This project develops an end-to-end Customer Churn Prediction & Retention Intelligence Analytics solution using Python, Machine Learning, and Power BI.

The solution predicts customer churn, identifies high-risk customers, analyzes churn drivers, segments customers based on churn probability, and provides actionable business recommendations through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

### Project Highlights

✔ Analyzed **1,409** telecom customers

✔ Identified **373** customers likely to churn

✔ Estimated churn rate of **26.47%**

✔ Calculated average monthly customer spending of **€64.35**

✔ Developed Machine Learning models for churn prediction

✔ Built a **4-page interactive Power BI Dashboard**

✔ Created a customer risk segmentation framework

✔ Generated business-oriented retention recommendations

# Business Problem

Customer attrition directly impacts revenue, profitability, and long-term customer lifetime value.

Telecommunication companies often identify churn only after customers have already left, making retention efforts ineffective and reactive rather than proactive.

The challenge addressed in this project is:

> How can a business identify customers likely to churn before they leave and take proactive actions to improve retention?

This project answers that question through predictive analytics and business intelligence.

# Major Business Insights

## Insight 1: Contract Type Strongly Influences Churn

Customers with month-to-month contracts exhibited significantly higher churn behavior compared to customers with one-year or two-year contracts.

### Business Recommendation

Encourage long-term contract adoption through:

- Loyalty discounts
- Contract upgrade incentives
- Bundle offers

---

## Insight 2: Higher Monthly Charges Increase Churn Risk

Customers paying higher monthly charges demonstrated a greater probability of churn.

### Business Recommendation

Introduce:

- Personalized pricing plans
- Service bundles
- Targeted promotional offers

---

## Insight 3: Long-Tenure Customers Are More Loyal

Customers with longer tenure showed stronger loyalty and significantly lower churn probability.

### Business Recommendation

Reward loyal customers through:

- Retention programs
- Loyalty benefits
- Exclusive service packages

---

## Insight 4: High-Risk Customers Require Immediate Attention

The model identified **373** customers classified as High Risk.

These customers represent approximately **26.47%** of the entire customer base.

### Business Recommendation

Implement:

- Personalized retention campaigns
- Dedicated customer support outreach
- Special retention offers
